Group A: Short Questions (2 Marks)

1. Differentiate between Legibility and Readability.
Legibility	                      Readability
Clarity of individual letters  	Ease of reading long text
Depends on font type & size	    Depends on spacing & layout
Letter-level concept	        Paragraph/text-level concept

2. What is the “Rule of Thirds” in layout design?

The Rule of Thirds divides a page into three equal horizontal and vertical sections. Important elements placed along these lines or intersections look more balanced and attractive.

3. Why should you limit the number of font families used on a website?

Limiting fonts:

Improves consistency

Enhances readability

Keeps design professional
Best practice: Use 2–3 font families only.

4. Define “Responsive Web Design.”

Responsive Web Design is a design approach where a website automatically adjusts its layout and content to fit different screen sizes and devices.

Group B: Long Questions (4 Marks)
5. Explain the “F-Pattern” of scanning and its influence on content placement.

Users scan web pages in an F-shaped pattern:

First scan horizontally at the top

Scan another horizontal line slightly lower

Finally scan vertically down the left side

Influence on design:

Place important content at the top

Keep headings and keywords on the left

Use short paragraphs and bullet points

6. Discuss the psychological impact of colors in web design.

Colors affect user emotions and decisions.

Blue: Trust, security, professionalism
Used for: Banks, corporate websites

Red: Urgency, attention, excitement
Used for: Sales, alerts, call-to-action buttons

Green: Calm, growth, positivity
Used for: Health, education, environment sites

7. Explain the “60-30-10 Rule” of color balance.

The 60-30-10 Rule ensures visual harmony:

60% – Primary color (background)

30% – Secondary color (content areas)

10% – Accent color (buttons, highlights)

Group C: Scenario-Based Questions (5 Marks)

8. Slow website due to high-resolution images

Image Optimization is important because it:

Reduces loading time

Improves user experience

Saves bandwidth

File formats:

JPEG: Best for photographs, smaller size

PNG: Best for logos & transparency, larger size

Solution: Compress and resize images; use JPEG for photos and PNG only when necessary.

9. Law firm design critique (Color Theory & Typography)

Problems:

Bright yellow causes eye strain and lacks seriousness

Comic Sans is informal and unprofessional

Violated principles:

Poor color psychology

Inappropriate typography

Better alternative:

Colors: Navy blue, gray, or white

Fonts: Serif fonts like Georgia or Times New Roman

10. Website unreadable on mobile

Missing Principle: Responsive Web Design

Fix using:

Media Queries: Apply styles based on screen size

Fluid Grids: Use percentage-based widths instead of fixed pixels

Result: Website adapts properly on all devices.
